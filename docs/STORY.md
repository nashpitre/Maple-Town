# I Created Maple Town

![](https://nashp.com/_media/2026/dashboard-sunny-20261025-67d5b1aa.png)

Inspired by our nearby area, I created Maple Town, a little dashboard for the family that brings the weather, calendar, and upcoming events together in one playful landscape.

![](https://nashp.com/_media/2026/dashboard-night-20261025-8d81471b.jpeg)

A friendly sun smiles and adapts to the weather and time of day. Clouds drift by, the sky grows darker when the weather changes, and the sun's rays fade away as the sun slowly turns into the moon during sunset.

![](https://nashp.com/_media/2026/dashboard-partly-cloudy-20261025-ecd6924d.jpeg)

Each family member’s face is a shortcut to their YouTube account, making it incredibly simple to get to their videos. Almost hidden at the bottom of the screen are a little app button and a settings button to access streaming services or make quick changes.

![](https://cdn.blot.im/folder/v-4abc1e37/blog_7d9c6729f90a4fd68ca68a09e88009f0/_media/2026/dashboard-rain-20261025-409e2e3a.png)

My old Apple TV was showing its age, and I was ready to replace it. But when Apple announced the new $300 Apple TV at twice the price, I took matters into my own hands. We mainly use our TV for YouTube, so I started brainstorming how I could solve this problem myself.

![](https://nashp.com/_media/2026/dashboard-storm-20261025-94194ec3.png)

After researching online for a week, I turned to Codex to help me build this project. By asking the simple question, “Can we get inside of this Samsung TV?”, the project steamrolled ahead.

![](https://cdn.blot.im/folder/v-3a9d7748/blog_7d9c6729f90a4fd68ca68a09e88009f0/_media/2026/dashboard-halloween-20261025-0b451da8.png)

After about four days of vibe coding, Maple Town is complete.
